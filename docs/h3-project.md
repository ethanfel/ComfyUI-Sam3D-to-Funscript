# H3 Animator project workspace

Open `workflows/03_h3_project.json`, set `folder_path` to the H3 Animator project directory, leave **operation → prepare**, and Run. Then click **Open H3 project workspace**. The node is **H3 Animator · Funscript Workspace** (`S3F_H3ProjectTimeline`). Restart ComfyUI once after installing the new node.

This reads `project.json`, the current page layouts and `index.json`. It includes only active pages/panels and completed takes with a `render.json` marker. Failed, unfinished and retired-layout renders are excluded. Refresh the project after generating new H3 videos. It never changes the H3 catalogue, prompts, pages or videos.

**Refresh H3 project** rereads active layouts, completed takes and image versions, even if H3 has not updated its index yet. A damaged page or take is listed under metadata issues; intact pages remain available. Invalid joined-sequence metadata blocks uncertain main-take processing until repaired. Latest takes are ordered by take number.

Pages and panels follow H3's reading order. The default view follows **Use as main** in H3 Animator, falling back to the newest eligible completed take. **All completed takes** includes the alternatives in the video selector. Active joined-panel (FLF) selections replace their source panels in the default view; excluded pages apply to either endpoint of a joined clip. The original panels remain available for drawing checks. Clean `video_clean.mp4` is preferred over the speech-bubble composite; a take appears only once. There is no random review order or Civitai view.

**Check drawings** works before videos exist: select a page and check its full image or a panel's clean reference. **Check current video** samples near 10%, 50% and 90% on CPU. Person boxes and scores appear on the images. Saved **Person confidence** applies to these checks and future Automatic runs; this project mode defaults to 0.15, while ordinary folder/video automatic processing keeps 0.4. Changing confidence invalidates cached person detections but does not overwrite completed tracking or manual edits. To retry a failed automatic region, remove or reconfigure that region in Timeline.

Very short videos show only the distinct frames available. Type a confidence value, then click **Save confidence** to apply it. Unsaved values survive background refreshes and failed saves; a successful save clears probe results from the previous threshold. Browsing pages or selecting panels does not load media. Click **Open main take** to switch editors; if saving the old clip fails, the open action stops and its unsaved editor is retained. Return to its page/panel to continue editing.

A person box is not proof of reliable 3D pose on drawings. Inspect **Edit person rectangles**, select the anchor and use **Preview anchor on this frame** in Timeline. Process a short range first and compare Motion Studio's curve with the visible movement. All ordinary tools remain available: crops, multiple anchors, masks, stabilization and orientation correction, curve editing, audio patterns, device preview, versions, ratings and intensity.

**Exclude page** excludes every take on that page, including future renders. **Exclude panel** applies to all current and future takes of that panel, including joined clips that use it. **Exclude video** affects that specific take. Exclusion is reversible and preserves drafts and exported scripts. Restore an excluded page before restoring an individually excluded video within it. Page/panel/video exclusions, presets and detector confidence are stored in `.s3f-h3.json` inside the project. No-content pages without videos can also be excluded.

An excluded take stays excluded if its preferred video changes between clean and composite. Invalid review settings produce an error and are left unchanged so they can be recovered.

The page/panel sidebar shows thumbnails, main-take status and saved-script coverage. Select panels, page ranges (for example `1–5, 8`) or all missing Main scripts, inspect **Processing queue**, then **Start selected**. Selection is independent of media loading. The queue skips still/camera-motion takes, excluded items, usable existing Main scripts and completed automatic drafts. Broken or missing L0 sidecars remain eligible for draft repair; valid optional axes can still be imported. **Include completed scripts / drafts** enables explicit bulk reprocessing and preserves Main plus a recovery version. Still-video takes remain available for manual editing; camera movement in a still is not body motion. It generates drafts for review; it never approves them automatically. The Show/Quality/Find filters affect review, not bulk eligibility. Remove waiting clips while another runs, or pause after the current clip. Removing a take in H3 does not stop the rest of the batch. The batch uses a labeled snapshot of selected takes; changing H3’s main selection does not silently substitute another take. Every waiting take is checked against the active layout before execution. You can also process or reprocess an individual clip through its Timeline.

Approval saves matching `.funscript` files alongside the selected video inside its take folder, with backups before replacement. For a clean video this is `video_clean.funscript` plus enabled axis suffixes. These scripts use that video's timeline; no chapter assembly or concatenation is implied. Approval checks the real clip timeline and FunCiv's 500,000-action limit before writing files. A one-key constant hold exports as two equal-position endpoints without changing the saved editor curve. **Save portable draft in project** stores editable Main curves, enabled axes, axis settings and review metadata under `.s3f-drafts/<panel>/<take>-<variant>.json`. **Restore portable draft** verifies the video’s SHA-256, preserves a recovery version and restores Main without approving or exporting sidecars. This works after moving the project or changing the ComfyUI output store. Source detections, masks and processing caches remain in ComfyUI’s normal output store; back up that store for complete editor sessions.

## Drawing trials, presets and loops

Video detector checks seek to three samples instead of decoding the full take, reuse one serialized CPU detector, and offer cancellation between samples/inference calls. A running model call finishes before cancellation takes effect. Image-only panels can still use box checks. The selected page/panel preset applies to checks and Automatic. H3's lower detector threshold also applies to temporal person association; stable low-confidence detections remain explicitly flagged for review. Ordinary video processing retains its existing confidence policy.

After **Check current video**, choose **Use this person**, select an anchor and **Test 2 seconds**. The trial goes through ComfyUI's model queue and runs real pose/curve extraction at 6 FPS in an isolated temporary workspace. Scrub the trial frames to inspect landmarks and compare the stroke curve. It never replaces Main or exports scripts. Cancel an executing trial through ComfyUI's queue controls. A successful short test does not establish accuracy for the rest of the clip; review the full result too.

**Processing presets & portable drafts** lets you load and save a project, page or panel preset. Confidence, preferred anchor, smoothing and sampling inherit in that order. Load a successful setup, choose another destination scope and save to copy it. With no H3 override, existing folder presets/node settings still apply. Pause the batch before changing presets so one batch has consistent settings.

For loop-marked takes, **Refresh checks** also compares endpoint positions and velocities on every enabled Main axis. Its seam warnings jump to the last and first parts of the clip for inspection in Motion Studio. These are review hints; no automatic seam correction modifies your curves.

H3 mode hides Civitai, Hugging Face publishing and unrelated bulk audio controls; local image tagging and the shared Timeline/Motion Studio remain available. After updating Python backend files, restart ComfyUI when your current jobs have finished, then reload the main ComfyUI tab. The workspace disables new backend actions until the updated H3 capability is available.

## FunCiv-player compatibility

FunCiv's manga reader uses the clean video when present, the panel's `main_take.json`, and six matching sidecars: `video_clean.funscript`, `.surge.funscript`, `.sway.funscript`, `.twist.funscript`, `.roll.funscript` and `.pitch.funscript`. When only the composite video exists, the stem is `video`. The exporter uses this same naming and clip-local integer-millisecond timing. Scripts are written on approval; editable drafts in ComfyUI's output store are not discoverable by the player. Refresh the book in FunCiv after saving scripts.

The FunCiv checkout rechecked later on 2026-09-25 now applies H3's `flf_sequence.json`, filters takes to the selected join, and handles cross-page endpoints. Its sequence tests and the six-axis export/import check pass. This resolves the player-side gap recorded in the first compatibility audit. Sam3D's exclusions only control funscript processing; they do not hide pages in FunCiv. Local ratings, intensity and notes are not imported by its manga reader.

Cross-check a checkout of the player with `node tests/test_h3_funciv.mjs /path/to/FunCiv-player /path/to/python`. This creates only a disposable neutral project, exports six axes through approval, and checks them through the player's real importer and motion compiler; it does not run a detector or connect hardware.
